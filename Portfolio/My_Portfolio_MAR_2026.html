Project 3: Telco Customer Churn

EDA and Predictive Model

In this end-to-end data science project, I developed a predictive model to tackle the $2.7$ trillion-dollar problem of customer churn using R and the Random Forest algorithm. Following the Google Data Analytics framework, I transformed raw telecommunications data into actionable business intelligence by identifying high-risk segments—specifically month-to-month contract holders and fiber optic users. By optimizing my model's classification threshold, I successfully increased churn detection sensitivity by 46%, providing a targeted retention list that prioritizes revenue protection over generic marketing. This project demonstrates my ability to bridge the gap between complex statistical modeling and strategic business recommendations.

Project 4: Online Retail

Growth & Inventory Analysis

In this project, I spearheaded a comprehensive data analysis for a UK-based online retailer to identify key growth drivers and optimize inventory management. Working with a large-scale dataset of over 540,000 transactions, I engineered a robust Python-based cleaning pipeline to handle significant data constraints, including the removal of over 135,000 missing customer records and the filtering of nearly 9,000 transaction anomalies like returns and adjustments. After transforming the data to ensure integrity, I performed advanced aggregations to reveal critical business insights: I uncovered a massive revenue surge in Q4 where sales nearly doubled compared to the yearly average, identified the 'Paper Craft Little Birdie' as the top revenue-generating product, and pinpointed the Netherlands and EIRE as the highest-potential international markets. I concluded the project by delivering three actionable recommendations—holiday inventory scaling, targeted international marketing, and an RFM-based loyalty program—designed to directly boost the retailer's net income and customer retention.

Project 5: Super Store

Revenue Analysis and Dashboards

This project presents an end-to-end sales analytics solution built using Microsoft SQL Server Management Studio and Microsoft Power BI to analyze a global retail dataset and generate actionable business insights. The analysis followed the structured six-step data analytics framework from the Google Data Analytics Certificate: Ask, Prepare, Process, Analyse, Share, and Act. Raw CSV data was first imported into a SQL Server database where extensive data cleaning and transformation were performed, including correcting data types, handling formatting inconsistencies, validating financial and date fields, and preparing the dataset for analysis using SQL queries. Analytical queries were then used to calculate key business metrics, identify revenue trends, analyze sales performance across regions and product categories, and evaluate the impact of discounts on profitability. The insights were communicated through interactive dashboards built in Power BI, including an Executive Overview, Sales Performance dashboard, and Profitability & Discount Analysis dashboard. These dashboards visualize key performance indicators such as total sales, total profit, profit margin, seasonal revenue trends, top-performing products, and discount-driven margin compression. The project concludes with data-driven business recommendations aimed at improving inventory planning, optimizing discount strategies, and focusing sales efforts on high-performing products and markets. This project demonstrates the full data analytics lifecycle—from raw data processing to business decision support—while showcasing practical skills in SQL, data modeling, data visualization, and analytical storytelling.
